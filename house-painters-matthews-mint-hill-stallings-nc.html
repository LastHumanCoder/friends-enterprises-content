Who is the best house painter in Matthews NC?+

The best house painter for your home is one that is fully insured, gives a written quote that spells out the prep, can show recent work nearby, and will tell you who is actually on site. Compare two or three written quotes for the same scope rather than choosing on price alone.

Do you work in Mint Hill and Stallings as well as Matthews?+

Yes. We paint homes across Matthews, Mint Hill and Stallings, along with Charlotte and the rest of the surrounding area. Our crew is based on Charlotte's east side, so all three towns are close by. A free walkthrough is usually easy to fit in within a few days.

Do I need HOA approval to repaint my house?+

In many Matthews and Stallings subdivisions, yes. Exterior color changes often go through an architectural review, and some neighborhoods approve only from a set palette. We can help you put together a scheme that fits your HOA's rules before you submit it.

How much does it cost to paint a house in Matthews or Mint Hill?+

It depends on the size of the house, the siding, how much prep is needed and the number of colors. A brick ranch with painted trim costs far less than a two-story home with full siding, so we quote each job after a free walkthrough. Painted trim, doors and shutters on brick is often the most affordable exterior refresh.

How long does an exterior repaint take?+

Most single-family exteriors take several days to about two weeks, depending on size, prep and the weather. We schedule around rain, temperature and dew so the paint has time to set properly. Prep takes the first day or two on most houses, and it is the part that decides how long the paint lasts.
