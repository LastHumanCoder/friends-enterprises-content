What we handle

Sanding, staining and repairs for worn hardwood floors

Most hardwood floors in Charlotte homes can be sanded and refinished several times, which makes hardwood floor refinishing one of the best value upgrades in a house. Good hardwood floor refinishing starts with repairs: loose or squeaky boards, damaged areas and gaps are fixed before the sander goes down. If you are wondering whether it is worth it, our guide on whether refinishing hardwood floors is worth it covers cost and value.

What a refinishing job with us includes

We start by checking how much wear layer the floor has left, whether any boards are loose, cupped or damaged, and whether the subfloor is sound. Then we quote the full job in writing, from repairs and sanding to the final coat. For new floors rather than refinishing, see our hardwood flooring service.

Sand and refinish

Full sanding to bare wood in stages, from coarse to fine grit, then stain if you want a new color and a durable topcoat. It removes scratches, gray patches and worn traffic lanes, and most solid hardwood can be refinished several times.

Squeaky floor repair

Squeaks usually come from boards or subfloor that have worked loose. We fasten them from above or below before sanding, because a squeaky floor under a new finish will crack the finish at the joints.

Stairs and treads

Hardwood stair treads take the most wear in a house. We sand and refinish treads, risers and railings to match the floors, or stain them in a contrasting color if you want the stairs to stand out.

Dark gray or black stains from water or pets often go deep into the wood. Light stains can sometimes be sanded out, while deeper ones mean replacing the affected boards and weaving new ones in.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Should I refinish hardwood floors myself or hire a pro?+

DIY can work in a small room if you rent a sander, practice first and are patient, but a drum sander can gouge a floor in seconds. A pro gets a flatter, more even result and knows how much wear layer is left to sand. Most homeowners hire out whole-house jobs.

How do you refinish hardwood floors?+

Repair loose or squeaky boards, sand the floor in stages from coarse to fine grit, vacuum thoroughly, apply stain if you want a new color, and finish with two or three coats of a durable topcoat, sanding lightly between coats. Edges and corners are sanded with a smaller edger.

How long does hardwood floor refinishing take?+

Most rooms take two to four days, depending on size, repairs and the finish you choose. Oil-based finishes take longer to cure than water-based ones. We give you a clear schedule before we start, including when furniture can go back.

Can you fix squeaky floors?+

Yes. Squeaks usually come from loose boards or subfloor rubbing against nails. We fasten them from above or below, and repair any damaged subfloor before refinishing, so the new finish stays intact.

Is there hardwood under my carpet?+

Often, especially in older Charlotte homes. We can lift a corner in a closet to check. If there is wood underneath in good enough shape, we pull the carpet, repair the boards and refinish them.

How often can hardwood floors be refinished?+

Solid hardwood can usually be refinished several times over its life, depending on how thick the wood is above the tongue. Engineered hardwood has a thinner wear layer and can often be refinished only once or twice. We check before we quote.
