FAQ

Quick answers.

Do you paint houses in east Charlotte?+

Yes. Our crew is based on Charlotte's east side, and we paint interiors and exteriors across the 28212 area, Hickory Grove, Eastway, Oakhurst and southeast Charlotte, along with the rest of the city. A free walkthrough is usually easy to schedule within a few days, since we are close by.

Can you repair drywall and paint in the same job?+

Yes, and it is the best way to do it. We repair cracks, holes and water damage first, match the texture, then prime and paint, so the repair disappears and you only need one contractor. It also keeps the schedule shorter, because there is no waiting between two different contractors.

Why do cracks keep coming back in my walls?+

Recurring cracks can point to movement in the framing or foundation, humidity changes or a failed earlier repair. We cut back and retape cracks properly, and we will tell you if a crack suggests something that needs a closer look first. Cracks at the corners of doors and windows are common in older homes and are usually cosmetic.

Do older east Charlotte homes need special prep?+

Often. Homes built before 1978 may have lead paint under newer coats, which needs lead-safe prep when it is sanded or scraped. Plaster walls also need different repair methods from drywall. We check the age of the house before quoting and explain what any special prep involves.

How much does drywall repair cost in east Charlotte?+

It depends on the size and number of repairs and whether texture needs matching. A few small patches cost far less than a wall rebuild after water damage, so we quote after seeing the damage. Water damage should always be traced and fixed at the source first, or the new drywall will fail too.

Do you offer discounts?+

Yes. We offer a 15% senior discount and 25% off exterior work. Every job starts with a free, written quote so you know the full cost before work begins. The discount is applied to the written quote, so you see the final number before agreeing to anything.
