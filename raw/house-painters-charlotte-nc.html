FAQ

Quick answers.

How do I find good house painters near me in Charlotte?+

Look for a local crew that is fully insured, gives a written quote that spells out the prep, and can show recent work in your area. Ask who will actually be on site, how they handle repairs before painting, and what paint they plan to use. Our guide to red flags when hiring a contractor covers the warning signs to watch for.

How much do house painters charge in Charlotte?+

It depends on the size of the job, the surfaces and how much prep is needed. A single room, a full exterior and a set of cabinets are priced very differently, and older homes often need more repair work. That is why we quote each job after a free walkthrough instead of giving a flat number over the phone.

How do I choose a painting contractor?+

Compare written quotes that cover the same scope, including prep, repairs and the number of coats. Ask about insurance, the products they use and how they protect your floors and furniture. Be wary of any quote far below the others, since the difference usually comes out of prep that you will not see until the paint fails.

Do you paint both interiors and exteriors?+

Yes. We paint interior walls, ceilings, trim and doors, exterior siding, trim, shutters and doors, and kitchen cabinets. We also handle drywall and carpentry repairs, washing and stain work, so a painting project does not need three different contractors to finish.

When is the best time to paint a house exterior in Charlotte?+

Spring and fall are usually best, and October is one of the most reliable months. Paint needs the air and surface above its minimum temperature, often 35F to 50F depending on the product, and time to set before evening dew. We schedule exterior work around the forecast rather than the calendar.
