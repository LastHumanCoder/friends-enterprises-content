Can you paint a house exterior in the fall?

Yes. Standard latex needs about 50F and low-temperature formulas are often rated to 35F. In Charlotte, October is usually one of the best exterior painting months, as long as painting stops early enough for the film to set before evening dew.

How much does popcorn ceiling removal cost?

Typically about $1 to $2 per square foot for removal alone, and about $2.50 to $5 with skim coating and paint. Homes built before the mid-1980s should have the texture tested for asbestos before anyone scrapes it.

Popcorn ceiling guide →

How much does it cost to paint kitchen cabinets?

A professional cabinet paint job for an average Charlotte kitchen typically costs about $3,500 to $7,000, depending on the number of doors and drawers and how the doors are finished. It is usually a fraction of the cost of new cabinets.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost?

A mid-range kitchen remodel in Charlotte typically costs about $25,000 to $75,000 and takes 6 to 12 weeks of work once materials arrive. Cabinets, counters and layout changes drive most of the cost.

Kitchen remodel guide →

What drives the cost of a bathroom remodel?

Seven factors: size and type of bathroom, layout changes, the shower or tub, tile, the vanity, flooring and fixtures. Moving the toilet or drain is usually the single biggest cost jump.

Bathroom cost guide →

How much does pressure washing cost?

Washing a typical house exterior usually costs about $300 to $700, a driveway about $100 to $300, and a deck about $200 to $500. Siding should always be soft washed with low pressure and a cleaner.

Pressure washing guide →

What drywall finish do I need?

Level 4 suits most walls with flat or eggshell paint. Level 5, a full skim coat, is for glossy paint, dark colors and walls hit by low-angle light. Skim coating typically costs about $1 to $3 per square foot.

Drywall finishes guide →

Is refinishing hardwood floors worth it?

Usually, if the wood has enough thickness left to sand. Refinishing costs far less than replacing floors and can make worn hardwood look close to new, often in two to four days per room.

FAQ

Quick answers.

What's a fair price for exterior house painting in North Carolina?+

For a typical one or two story home in the Charlotte area, a full exterior repaint often costs about $3,000 to $7,500. Size, siding type, the amount of prep and repair, and the number of colors move the price. A fair quote is written, lists the prep, and names the paint being used.

How soon can a painting company start a project in Charlotte NC?+

It depends on the season. In the busy spring and fall months, many painting companies are booked one to several weeks out. Interior work is often easier to schedule in winter. Booking your walkthrough early is the best way to get the dates you want. For exterior work, the weather adds its own delays, so leave some room in your plans.

Who offers the best warranty on exterior painting in Charlotte?+

Warranties vary a lot between contractors. Ask each company to put its warranty in writing, including how long it lasts, what it covers, such as peeling or blistering, and what it excludes. Compare those terms alongside the paint products and prep each quote includes.

What's a reasonable timeline to get a painting quote in Charlotte NC?+

A walkthrough can usually be arranged within a few days to a week, and a written quote should follow shortly after. If a company cannot tell you what is in the quote, or will not put it in writing, keep looking. Most quotes for a single room or a full exterior should not take more than a week or two from first call to written number.

How do I find a trustworthy contractor for home renovations near me?+

Look for a local contractor who is insured, gives detailed written quotes, can show recent work and references, and does not ask for most of the money up front. In North Carolina, larger projects require a licensed general contractor. Our guide to red flags when hiring a contractor covers the warning signs.
