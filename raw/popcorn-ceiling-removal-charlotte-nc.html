FAQ

Quick answers.

How much does popcorn ceiling removal cost in Charlotte?+

It depends on the size of the room, whether the texture has been painted, the ceiling height and the finish you want. Our guide to removing popcorn ceiling lists typical per square foot ranges for removal, skim coating and paint. We give a fixed written quote after seeing the ceiling, so you know the full cost up front.

Do you test for asbestos before removing popcorn ceiling?+

We recommend a lab test for any popcorn ceiling in a home built before the mid-1980s, and we will tell you plainly if yours needs one. If the test is positive, removal must be done by a licensed abatement contractor, or the ceiling can be covered with new drywall instead of scraped.

Can you remove popcorn ceiling that has been painted?+

Yes, but it takes longer, because paint seals the texture and stops water from softening it. In some rooms a skim coat over the existing texture is faster and gives a better result, and we will explain the choice before we quote.

How long does popcorn ceiling removal take?+

Most single rooms take two to three days, because each coat of joint compound has to dry before the next and before paint. A whole house is scheduled room by room, so you can keep living in the home while the work moves through it.

Will popcorn ceiling removal be messy?+

Removal is messy, which is why we cover floors and walls in plastic, close off vents and remove or protect light fixtures before starting. Debris is bagged as we go, and the room is cleaned fully before we hand it back. Most homeowners are surprised how clean the room is at the end.

Should I get a smooth ceiling or a new texture?+

Smooth ceilings look the most modern but show flaws under strong light. Knockdown and orange peel hide small imperfections and cost a little less to finish. We can show you samples and recommend the best fit for the room and your lighting.
